# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in jgrep to be welcoming, professional, and
free from harassment. This applies to project spaces, events, and other
interactions that represent the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when reviewing code or discussing
  technical disagreements.
- Assume good faith, ask for clarification, and focus criticism on ideas and
  contributions.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help keep discussions accessible to people with different backgrounds and
  levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hateful or discriminatory language, sexualized content,
deliberate intimidation, trolling, and sharing private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when behavior conflicts with this policy. To report a concern,
request a private contact channel through the repository without posting
sensitive details. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
